Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • AQUENZA (Bijnor, Uttar Pradesh)

1. Introduction

AQUENZA ("we," "our," or "us") operates a packaged-water supply coordination and custom-branding agency service in Bijnor, Uttar Pradesh. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, handle, and safeguard business and customer information when you use our website, submit an enquiry, or interact with our digital QR bottle destinations.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you submit a quotation request or contact our team:

  • Contact Details: Customer name, business/venue name, telephone/mobile number, and optional email address.
  • Service Requirements: Selected bottle sizes (250ml, 500ml, 1 Litre), estimated order quantities, custom branding requirements, and delivery location details.
  • Brand Assets: Logos, vector artwork, brand color preferences, and menu links provided by participating businesses for bottle label printing and QR destination pages.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used solely for lawful commercial operations:

  • To prepare and issue itemized quotations and order specifications.
  • To communicate via phone or WhatsApp regarding sample proofing and delivery logistics.
  • To build, publish, and host customer digital QR destinations as agreed upon with participating businesses.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your contact or business data to third-party advertisers.

4. Digital QR Destinations & Guest Privacy

When dining guests scan QR codes on bottles, no personal tracking cookies or invasive device identifiers are harvested. Feedback submitted via our demonstration feedback forms is processed strictly for venue service improvement.
