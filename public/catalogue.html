Mandatory Commercial Printing Measurements Checklist

To prevent production errors, all label designs (200 ml, 500 ml, 1 Litre) must be calibrated to the physical dimensions of the partner bottler's blow-molded bottles before commercial print execution.

Specification Parameter Status in Concept Measurement Required Before Commercial Print Compliance / Verification Role
Label Height & Width (Die Line) Approximate visual mockup Exact millimeter circumference and label height from supplier bottle mold Prevents label overlap or gap on curved bottle waist
Substrate & Adhesive Photographed sample verified Waterproof BOPP film with moisture-resistant chill-safe adhesive Ensures labels do not peel or wrinkle when stored in commercial chillers
Mandatory Declarations Zone Layout planned Partner FSSAI License No., BIS / ISI mark dimensions, Batch / Date / MRP print box Mandatory under Food Safety and Legal Metrology Packaged Commodities Rules
QR Code Print Resolution High-res vector / 300+ DPI Minimum 18mm x 18mm quiet zone for optical scan reliability in dim ambient dining light Guarantees 100% scan success rate on smartphone cameras
