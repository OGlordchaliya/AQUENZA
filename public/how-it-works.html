Step-by-Step Flow

Our 5-Step Working Process

A transparent, systematic pipeline that turns your venue branding concept into ready-to-serve bottled water.

1

Select Bottle Format

Determine the best bottle size for your establishment's needs:

  • 200 ml Bottle: Banquets, weddings, conferences, single-serve catering.
  • 500 ml Bottle: Casual dining, café tables, hotel guest rooms.
  • 1 Litre Custom Bottle: Fine dining, VIP banquet tables, flagship own-brand presence.
2

Collect Customer Brand Details

Provide your brand elements:

  • High-resolution venue logo (PNG, PDF, or vector format)
  • Exact brand colors, venue tagline, and preferred aesthetic
  • Target digital QR destination (menu link, map link, or custom promo page)
3

Design & Print Label + QR Destination

We generate digital proof mockups for your approval before printing:

  • Alignment with supplier bottle mold circumference & height
  • Inclusion of all statutory labelling declarations
  • Moisture-resistant chill-safe printing on commercial grade BOPP film
4

Quality & Seal Verification

A multi-checkpoint verification before batch release:

  • Inspection of factory tamper-evident seals and caps
  • Physical QR code optical scan verification using multiple mobile devices
  • Label adhesion integrity under refrigeration simulation
5

Packaging & Reliable Local Delivery

Clean, shrink-wrapped cases dispatched right to your venue in Bijnor:

  • Dust-protected case packaging
  • Coordinated delivery scheduling matching restaurant prep times
  • Dedicated support desk contactable anytime via 8630071125 & 8755306639
