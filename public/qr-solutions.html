Engineered For Stability & Multi-Tenant Security

To ensure commercial viability for hospitality operators, our QR ecosystem is architected around two core engineering principles:

1. Permanent URL Persistence

When hundreds or thousands of bottles are printed and distributed, the physical QR code on the bottle cannot be recalled. We use stable, permanent slug URLs (e.g. aquenza.in/v/the-grand-heritage). The venue can modify their menu, operating hours, or chef specials anytime in the cloud without ever reprinting their physical bottles.

2. Multi-Tenant Account Security

In our production roadmap, each participating client receives a secure, tokenized account with role-based access control (RBAC). Strict database tenant isolation prevents one restaurant owner from viewing or modifying another business’s menu, analytics, or private customer feedback.

Implementation Transparency Notice: The customer venue page provided on this website (The Grand Heritage) is an interactive demonstration prototype created to showcase prospective clients how their venue will look. Live multi-tenant database authentication and merchant administrative dashboards will be provisioned in Phase 2 upon onboarding verified commercial accounts.
